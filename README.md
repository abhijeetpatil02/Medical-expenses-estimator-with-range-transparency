## 📌 About the Project

**PreCare** is a web-based medical cost transparency tool that helps patients understand the potential financial impact of healthcare procedures **before** they visit a facility. Instead of being surprised by a hospital bill, users can get a clear picture of **low, average, and high price ranges** for medical procedures, treatments, and consultations.

The tool takes into account:
- 📍 **Location** — Costs vary significantly by region
- 🏨 **Provider Type** — Private clinic vs. government hospital vs. specialist
- 🛡️ **Insurance Status** — With or without insurance coverage

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔐 **User Authentication** — Secure login and signup system
- 📊 **Cost Range Transparency** — Shows low, average, and high estimates (not just a single number)
- 🤖 **ML-Powered Predictions** — Machine learning model trained on real medical expense data
- 📍 **Location-Aware Estimates** — Adjusts predictions based on geographic region
- 🏥 **Provider Type Filtering** — Government, private, and specialist cost breakdowns
- 💳 **Insurance Impact View** — Compare costs with and without insurance
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Works seamlessly on desktop and mobile devices
